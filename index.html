Up & Down the River

1. Top of the river

The most cards dealt in a hand. You play up to it, play it three times, then come back down.

2. Players

Start with yourself as Player 1 (the score keeper), then go around the table in seating order. A random player is picked to deal first, and the deal moves one seat each hand.
